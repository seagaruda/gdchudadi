Single-player Cantonese Big Two (Chudadi / Big Two) in your pocket — fully offline, no ads, no sign-up.

CLASSIC 4-PLAYER RULES
Jokers removed, 13 cards each, counter-clockwise play. Legal plays are singles, pairs, triples and five-card hands (straight flush > four of a kind > full house > flush > straight). The player holding the 3 of diamonds leads the first deal; the winner of each deal leads the next.

AI THAT ACTUALLY COUNTS CARDS
Your three computer opponents don't just follow suit. They track what has been played, count points, decide when to beat a hand and when to hold back — and they will gang up on whoever is ahead. To win points off them, you have to think.

SCORING
Points are counted from the cards left in each loser's hand: 0-7 cards x1, 8-9 x2, 10-12 x3, 13 x4, doubled again if the 2 of spades is still held. Settled pairwise, winner doubled.

WHY PLAY
- Fully offline: works on the subway, on a plane, anywhere
- No ads, no in-app purchases, no personal data collected
- One-handed portrait play, a deal takes a few minutes
- Hand-type hints and built-in rules, so beginners can start and veterans can dig in
- Local Cantonese conventions: single-card calls, top-card blocks, dragon straights

WHO IT'S FOR
Anyone who can't find three friends for a game; anyone who wants to sharpen their Big Two play; anyone who just wants a quiet hand without ads.
